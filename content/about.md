Wavey is an open-source RF sensing system designed for passive environmental monitoring.

Our goal is to make spatial awareness accessible, privacy-preserving, and affordable using consumer-grade WiFi hardware. By leveraging WiFi Channel State Information (CSI), Wavey enables occupancy detection, motion tracking, and behavioral inference without cameras, radar, or wearables.
